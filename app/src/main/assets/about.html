Genesis

Awakening The Natural Light Within

Lumière Beauty was established in 2021 with an uncompromising mission: to banish toxic fillers and heavy synthetics from the luxury beauty landscape, replacing them with sensory, high-potency organic botanicals.

Our team of Parisian cosmetic chemists works closely with regenerative rose fields in Grasse and sustainable olive groves in Provence. Every drop of our formulas is bio-fermented to ensure molecular compatibility with all skin tones and sensitivity profiles.

We believe luxury beauty is not about masking who you are; it is an intimate ritual of honoring your true radiance, celebrating every curve, shade, and contour of your complexion.

"Beauty is neither vanity nor an illusion. It is the palpable vibration of feeling cherished in your own skin. When a woman catches her reflection in the golden morning light and smiles, that is the Lumière glow."

Camille Laurent

Founder & Creative Director
