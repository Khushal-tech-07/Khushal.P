Frequently Asked Inquiries

Are Lumière Beauty formulations suitable for ultra-sensitive skin?

Yes, unconditionally. Every product is dermatologically tested on reactive and allergy-prone skin profiles. Our formulas omit synthetic fragrances, essential oil allergens, parabens, and micro-plastics.

How long does domestic and international delivery take?

Orders are packaged with biodegradable pink mulberry tissue and dispatched within 24 hours. Express domestic delivery arrives in 2-3 business days, while global air freight delivers in 4-6 business days.

What is your return & satisfaction policy?

We want you to adore your daily ritual. If a formulation does not bring light to your complexion, you may return it within 30 days for a complimentary exchange or full refund with free prepaid courier pickup.

Are your containers recyclable or refillable?

Yes. All our flacons are engineered from 100% recyclable French flint glass. We offer discounted refill cartridges for our lipsticks and moisturizer jars to minimize single-use waste.
